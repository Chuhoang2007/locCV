# Phase 5 — Decisions, Inbox and account data

Mode: Operate. Precise extension of the established recruiting flow in docs/04_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md §9. Preserve DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json.

THESIS: HR closes a reviewed application with one clear, final response; candidates can find that response and distinguish it from automated scoring.

OWN-WORLD: Existing Be Vietnam Pro, pine controls, warm canvas, thin separators, native fields and visible keyboard focus. Flat message rows join the existing application lists; no imagery or new tokens.

STORY: Review evidence, choose a decision, write a personal message, confirm inline, then read the persisted response. Open Inbox, identify unread messages, read one and follow its application link. Settings offers an explicit deletion request with a seven-day cancellation period and explains closed jobs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: HR decision section follows the application heading and precedes evidence; final feedback occupies the same region. Inbox leads with its heading, unread count, all/unread links and a chronological row list. Message detail shows job context, subject, date, escaped body and application link. Settings retains its established composition.

FORM: Code-led extension of fully specified workflows; no seed or concept round. Signature interaction: inline confirmation repeats the exact decision/message, while a conflict offers refresh rather than overwriting another HR action.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
